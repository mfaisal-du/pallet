A2 · 3 min

🏭 Register a New Pallet (Factory)

Creating a pallet live is the strongest opening move — it shows the system is real, not a mock-up.

1

Open the Register page

Click Register Pallet in the left menu.

Expect: A registration form with material, dimensions, capacity, cost and a live QR preview.
2

Fill the form

Enter a memorable number such as DEMO-PLT-002 (or any number you like), choose Wood, dimensions 1200×800×150, capacity 1000 kg, cost 15, and pick today's date.

"Each pallet gets a unique number and a QR code. When the client's staff scan it with any phone, they instantly see where the pallet is." Expect: A QR code preview appears with the pallet number under it.
3

Save the pallet

Click Register Pallet to save.

Expect: A success confirmation with the QR code, and a "Print label" option. Click Print label to show the printable tag with QR — great for factory staff.
💡
Trainer tip: Mention that labels can be printed and stuck on pallets. Staff just scan to move pallets — no paperwork, no typing.
A3 · 8 min

📦 Full Cycle via Single Scan

The most important part of the demo. Walk one pallet from warehouse to customer and back, scanning at every step.

ℹ️
Use the pallet you just created (DEMO-PLT-002) — or reuse DEMO-PLT-001 from the demo data.
1

Warehouse — Load products

Click Scan in the menu. Type the pallet number into the scan box and click the search button.

Expect: The pallet is found — status Available, next action Load Products.

Add a product line (e.g. product Water 5L, qty 40), then click Mark as Loaded.

"The warehouse team scans the pallet and loads it with products. The system remembers exactly what's on each pallet." Expect: Status changes to Loaded.
2

Dispatcher — Send it out

On the same Scan page (or the Dispatch page), scan the same pallet again. Fill the dispatch form: truck Y-5796, driver Faisal, destination AL-NAHDA STORE.

"Now the dispatcher assigns the pallet to a specific truck and driver and sends it to a customer. This creates a trip." Expect: Status changes to In Transit with the truck, driver and destination shown.
3

Delivery — Customer confirms

Scan the pallet again, enter the receiver's name in the delivery form, and confirm.

"The customer's delivery receiver confirms receipt with their own name. That's proof of delivery — no more disputes." Expect: Status changes to Delivered.
4

Return pickup — Collector

Scan the same pallet again. The return form shows Collector automatically filled with the logged-in user.

"When the truck visits the customer to collect the empty pallet, the collector's name is filled automatically. They scan once — the pallet starts coming home." Expect: Status changes to Returning.
5

Factory receive — Inspector

Scan the same pallet once more. The form shows Inspector pre-filled. Confirm to receive it back.

"Back at the factory, the inspector receives the pallet. It's now available for the next trip — ready to be reused." Expect: Status returns to Available. The trip is counted.
6

Show the full history

Click Pallets, then click the pallet number to open its profile.

"Every single scan is on the timeline — who did what, when, and with what note. This pallet's whole journey is 100% traceable." Expect: The 360° profile shows the movement timeline (Load → Dispatch → Deliver → Return Pickup → Factory Receive), trip count = 1, and the QR code.
A4 · 8 min

📚 Batch Scanning (Trips) — Multiple Pallets, One Truck

Show that the client doesn't scan pallets one-by-one for dispatch: they create ONE trip and scan many pallets onto it.

1

Open Batch Scan

Click Batch Scan in the menu.

"For dispatches with many pallets, we create one trip — one truck, one driver, one destination — then scan pallets onto it one after another. One trip, many pallets." Expect: The batch scan page with a list of trips and a New Trip button.
2

Create a Dispatch Trip

Click New Trip, choose Dispatch Trip, pick truck UU-123456, driver Usman Wazir, destination AL-NAHDA STORE, then Start Trip.

Expect: The trip opens with truck/driver/destination locked in the header, and a scan box to add pallets.
3

Scan pallets onto the trip

Scan TEST-PLT-001, then PT-MS26RRQTOQJG, then your new DEMO-PLT-002 (if it's Available).

"Each pallet is added to the same manifest. The counter shows '3 scanned'. Truck and driver are entered only once — huge time saving for busy dispatchers." Expect: Manifest count increments; each pallet's status flips from Available to In Transit.
4

Close the trip

Click Complete Trip.

"The trip is closed — the whole batch is out for delivery under one driver." Expect: Trip status becomes Closed with the final manifest count.
5

Return collection batch

Create a new trip, choose Return Collection. Notice Collector is pre-filled with your name.

"On the return side it works the same way: the collector creates one return trip and scans all the empty pallets being picked up from customers." Expect: Return Collection trip opens with Collector pre-filled; scanning delivered pallets moves them to Returning.
6

Factory receive batch

Create a third trip, choose Factory Receive — Inspector is pre-filled. Scan the returning pallets, then complete the trip.

"At the factory, the inspector receives the whole batch back in one go. Everything is available again for the next trip." Expect: All scanned pallets return to Available, and each pallet's trip count increases.
💡
Client hook: Ask "How many pallets do you send per day?" — then say "With batch scanning, one person can dispatch a hundred pallets in minutes."

Show the combined capability

Open Batch Scan → New Trip.

4

How the admin grants roles

"The admin simply ticks the roles each person needs. Multi-select, saved instantly — every role change is audited." Expect: The edit dialog shows all 8 roles as checkboxes with the message "Pick one or more — the user can combine roles."
⚠️
Honest note for the client: combining roles is fully supported, but for bigger companies, keeping roles separate is better practice for control (auditors like "one person can't do everything"). The system supports both styles.
Part C · Client Q&A Cheat-Sheet
C · Ready Answers

❓ Questions Clients Usually Ask

Confident answers — all of these are implemented and working in the live app.

Client questionAnswer you give
"Can we change the pallet status labels?" "Yes — the admin can rename statuses and even add new ones from Settings → Status Labels. The new labels appear everywhere across the pallet's whole lifecycle automatically."
"Can the same person dispatch AND receive deliveries?" "Yes. One user can be granted several roles — e.g. Dispatcher + Delivery Receiver + Return Collector. You saw it live in Part B."
"Is the truck & driver module fully working — add, edit, delete?" "Yes — full CRUD from the Fleet page: add trucks and drivers, edit details, and delete unused ones. Dispatches link to real fleet records, so reports merge everything under the driver's name."
"Can we send many pallets with one truck, not one pallet at a time?" "Absolutely — that's Batch Scanning (Part A4). Create one trip with the truck/driver/destination, then scan as many pallets as you want onto it."
"On returns, can the collector and inspector be the logged-in user automatically?" "Yes — on the return flow, the Collector name and the factory Inspector name are pre-filled from the logged-in user, once, and you can scan many pallets after that."
"Do we get proof of delivery?" "Yes — the delivery receiver confirms with their name, and it's recorded on the pallet timeline with date & time. You can pull it up any time."
"What if a pallet is damaged or lost?" "Damage is recorded with description + photo and goes through a repair workflow (Begin Repair → Complete Repair) with full history. Lost pallets are flagged and appear in the Lost Pallets report."
"How do we know how our drivers perform?" "The Driver Performance report shows trips, dispatches and deliveries per driver. You also get utilization and return-performance reports — all exportable to Excel."

🛟 Troubleshooting & Preparation Checklist

Do this BEFORE the client arrives. 5 minutes.

  • App is running: confirm http://localhost:3001 loads in the browser.
  • Both logins work: admin + multirole (password password123).
  • Demo pallets exist: DEMO-PLT-001, TEST-PLT-001, PT-MS26RRQTOQJG are Available.
  • Fleet exists: trucks Y-5796 & UU-123456, drivers Faisal & Usman Wazir.
  • Close the QR/print dialogs before scanning the next pallet.
  • If a scan "does nothing": the pallet may need a product line before Load, or the form may have a required field — read the on-screen message.
  • If a pallet can't be scanned: check its status — e.g. a Delivered pallet can only be Return-picked, not loaded. That's the lifecycle working correctly.
⛔
If something really breaks: don't improvise in front of the client — move to the next section of the demo and check the issue afterwards. The pallet you create today stays in the system and can be reused.
