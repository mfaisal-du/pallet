1. System Overview

What PalletTrack Pro does and how it fits into daily operations

PalletTrack Pro is a real-time web application that tracks returnable pallets through the entire water delivery cycle. Every pallet has a unique QR code. Staff scan the code at each handoff point — the system records who did what, when, and where.

ℹ️ Key principle: Every status change (load, dispatch, deliver, return) must be done through a QR scan on the Scan page. This is how the system knows where every pallet is at any moment.

What the system tracks:

  • 📦 Pallet identity — number, QR, material, dimensions
  • 🔄 Full movement history — every scan logged with user + timestamp
  • 🚛 Fleet assignment — which truck and driver carried each pallet
  • ⚠️ Damage tracking — damage records + repair status
  • 📊 Reports & audit — full history exportable by date/status
  • 🔔 Notifications — alerts for damaged, lost, overdue pallets

4. Factory: Registering New Pallets

How the manufacturing team adds new pallets into the system

👤 Log in as Manufacturing — manufacturing@pallettrack.local / password123
1

Go to the Pallets page

In the left sidebar, click Pallets. You will see the pallets list with filter chips at the top.

2

Click "Register Pallet"

Click the green + Register Pallet button in the top right. A modal will open.

3

Fill in the pallet details

Pallet Number: Leave blank for auto-generation (recommended) or type a custom code like PLT-001.

Manufacture Date: Select today's date.

Material Type: Choose from the dropdown (plastic, wood, metal, etc.).

Dimensions: e.g. 1200×800×150mm

Weight Capacity: e.g. 500 kg

Quantity: Use the +/− buttons or preset (1, 5, 10, 25) to register multiple at once.

4

Click "Generate QR Code"

The pallet(s) are created with status Available and the timeline records "Registered at factory".

5

Print the QR Label

On the Pallets page, find your new pallet and click the 🖨️ Print button. The label shows the QR code, pallet number, material type, and company branding. Print and attach to the physical pallet.

6

Alternatively: Use Register Pallet page

The sidebar also has a Register Pallet menu item (for manufacturing + admin only) which opens a dedicated full-page registration form with a live QR preview.

ℹ️ After registering, the pallet appears on the Dispatch page → Step 1 (Available — Scan to Load). It stays there until a Warehouse Loader scans it to mark it as Loaded.

5. Warehouse: Loading Pallets onto a Truck

The loader scans pallets to mark them as loaded and ready for dispatch

👤 Log in as Warehouse Loader — loader@pallettrack.local / password123
1

Go to the Scan page

Click Scan in the sidebar. You will see the QR scanner view.

2

Scan the pallet QR code

Point the camera at the pallet's QR label. Or switch to Manual mode and type the pallet number (e.g. PT-MS24JRRVQ46N). The system will look it up and show the pallet details.

3

Select "Load" action

A panel appears showing the pallet's current status (Available) and the available actions. Click Load onto truck.

Optionally fill: products loaded, notes.

4

Submit

Click Confirm Action. Status changes to Loaded. The pallet now appears in the Dispatch page → Step 2 (Ready to Dispatch).

🚛

6. Dispatcher: Sending Pallets Out

The dispatcher assigns the truck and driver and marks the pallet as in-transit

👤 Log in as Dispatcher — dispatcher@pallettrack.local / password123
1

Go to Dispatch page (optional view)

Click Dispatch in sidebar. The Dispatch Pipeline tab shows two sections: pallets that still need loading (Step 1) and pallets ready to dispatch (Step 2). This is read-only — actual dispatch is done via scanning.

2

Go to Scan page and scan the loaded pallet

Scan the pallet QR code. The system detects its status is Loaded and shows the available next action: Dispatch.

3

Fill dispatch details

Truck Number: Select from the fleet dropdown (pre-registered trucks). Selecting a registered truck links the dispatch to that fleet record.

Driver Name: Select from the driver dropdown. Selecting a registered driver links the dispatch to their fleet record — used by the Driver Performance Report and fleet history.

Destination: Type the customer/delivery site name.

Expected Delivery: Select the expected delivery date.

Notes: Optional instructions.

4

Submit

Click Confirm Action. Status changes to In Transit. The pallet moves to the "In Transit" tab in the Dispatch page showing truck and driver info.

7. Delivery Receiver: Confirming Delivery

The receiver at the customer site scans the pallet to confirm it arrived

👤 Log in as Delivery Receiver — receiver@pallettrack.local / password123
1

Open the Scan page (works on mobile)

The receiver can use any phone or tablet connected to the company network. Open the app, log in, go to Scan.

2

Scan the pallet QR code

Point the camera at the pallet label. The system shows pallet details and status In Transit.

3

Select "Deliver" and fill receiver info

Receiver Name: Name of the person accepting the pallet at the customer site.

Receiver Contact: Phone number or ID.

Condition: Note any visible condition issues.

4

Submit

Status changes to Delivered. The pallet is now at the customer site. The system records who confirmed delivery and when.

9. Return Flow: Getting Pallets Back

Two-step return: collector picks up from customer, factory receiver signs them in

Step A — Return Collector picks up from customer site

👤 Log in as Return Collector — collector@pallettrack.local / password123
1

Scan pallet at customer location

The pallet is Delivered. Select Return Pickup. The Collector field is auto-filled with your logged-in user name (editable) — fill it only if a different person is physically collecting. Choose the condition and submit → status becomes Returning.

Damaged at pickup? Choose Damaged under condition and describe it in the notes — the system records the pickup and logs a damage report automatically, so the damage is on record before the pallet reaches the factory.

Step B — Factory Receiver signs pallet back in

👤 Log in as Factory Receiver — factory@pallettrack.local / password123
2

Scan pallet at factory gate

Pallet is Returning. Select Receive at Factory. The Inspector field is auto-filled with your logged-in user name (editable). Fill the condition checklist. Submit → status returns to Available. Trip count increments by 1.

ℹ️ The pallet is now available for the next delivery cycle. Check the pallet profile to see the full trip history.

10. Damaged & Repair Workflow

What to do when a pallet is broken or needs inspection

1

Mark as Damaged (Scan page)

Any role with scan access can mark a pallet damaged. Scan the pallet → select Mark Damaged → describe the damage → submit. Status becomes Damaged. A damage record is created automatically (visible in the Damaged Pallet Report) and a notification is sent to all admins and managers.

2

Begin Repair (Pallet Profile page)

Admin or Manager opens the pallet profile (from Pallets list). Click Begin Repair action. Status becomes Under Repair.

3

Complete Repair

When the physical pallet is fixed, click Complete Repair on the profile. Status returns to Available and the pallet re-enters the normal cycle. All open damage reports for the pallet are marked Resolved automatically, and completing a repair does not count as a completed trip.

4

Retire a pallet permanently

If a pallet is beyond repair, an Administrator can click Retire. Status becomes Retired and the pallet is removed from active counts but kept in audit history.

12. Command Center

Live operations hub — real-time pallet counts, activity feed, alerts, and quick lookup

Available to: Administrator Manager

1

Pipeline counters (top section)

Shows live count for every status — Available, Loaded, In Transit, Delivered, Returning. Updates every 30 seconds automatically. You can also click the refresh button.

2

Pallet Lookup — Type or Scan

Toggle between Type (enter pallet number) and Scan QR (use camera). Once found, a full details card shows status, material, dimensions, trip count, location, and a direct link to the pallet profile — without any extra clicks.

3

Live Activity Feed

Shows every scan action across the system in real time with who did it and when. The sparkline chart shows scan frequency over the past 24 hours.

4

Alerts panel

Lists pallets that are overdue (return date passed), damaged, or lost. Click any row to open that pallet's profile immediately.

13. Fleet Management

Register trucks and drivers, and assign drivers to trucks

Available to: Administrator Manager Dispatcher

1

Add a Truck

Go to Fleet → Trucks tab → click Add Truck. Enter the plate number, vehicle model, capacity (number of pallets), and optional notes. Click Save.

2

Add a Driver

Switch to the Drivers tab → click Add Driver. Enter name, phone number, and license number. Click Save.

3

Assign a Driver to a Truck

In the Trucks table, click the 🔗 Assign icon next to any truck. A modal appears — select the driver from the dropdown. Click Assign Driver. The truck card now shows the driver's name and phone.

This assignment is a linked database relation — a truck can only be assigned a driver that exists in the Drivers list, so fleet records stay consistent. The assignment carries over to Dispatch — the dispatcher sees which driver is default for each truck.

4

Deactivate a Truck or Driver

Click the power icon (⏻) next to a truck or driver to deactivate. Inactive vehicles/drivers do not appear in the dispatch dropdown. Reactivate at any time.

15. System Settings

Configure operational thresholds, label branding, and material types

Available to: Administrator Manager

1

Return Window (days)

The number of days a pallet can be out before it is flagged as overdue. Default is 14 days. Change this to match the company's SLA with customers.

2

Low Inventory Threshold

When the count of Available pallets drops below this number, the Dashboard will highlight it. Default is 50.

3

Material Types

Add or remove pallet material types that appear in the Register Pallet dropdown. Click + to add a new type (e.g. "fiberglass"). The new type appears immediately when registering pallets. The system auto-saves when you add a type.

4

Label & Branding

Change the company name, tagline, accent colour, and footer text that prints on pallet QR labels. A live preview shows how the label will look before saving.

16. Training Reset — Clean Up Test Data

How to reset the system between training sessions without losing users or settings

🚨 Admin only. These actions permanently delete data. Only use during training/testing — never on a live production database that contains real operational data.
1

Go to Settings → Database & Testing section

Scroll to the bottom of the Settings page. You will see three action cards.

2

Option A: Seed Demo Pallets

Click Seed Demo Data. A confirmation dialog appears — type SEED and click Confirm. Creates 10 pallets across all statuses so trainees see a realistic system without manually going through every step first.

3

Option B: Clear All Pallet Data

Click Clear Pallets. Type DELETE in the confirmation dialog and click Confirm. Removes all pallets, movements, and damage records. Users and settings are preserved. Use this to start a fresh training session.

4

Option C: Clear Audit Log

Type CLEAR to wipe audit trail entries. Useful if you want the audit log to only show activity from the current training session.

💡 Recommended training session flow: (1) Clear pallets → (2) Optionally seed demo data → (3) Walk through each role's workflow with trainees → (4) Clear pallets again to reset for next group.

18. Batch Scanning — Save Once, Scan Many

Create one trip header, then scan multiple pallets onto it — no re-typing per pallet

ℹ️ What batch scanning does: For Dispatch, Return Collection and Factory Receive, you now create a Trip first — entering the truck, driver and destination (or collector / inspector) once — and then scan as many pallets onto that trip as you like. The trip header is saved onto every pallet automatically.
👤 Available to: Administrator Dispatcher Return Collector Factory Receiver — Manager is view-only.
1

Open the Batch Scan page

Click Batch Scan in the sidebar. You'll see a list of open trips and a + New Trip button.

2

Choose the trip type

Pick Dispatch Trip, Return Collection Trip, or Factory Receive Trip.

3

Fill the header once

Dispatch: select the Truck and Driver from the fleet dropdowns and type the Destination.

Return Collection: the Collector name is auto-filled with your logged-in user — just verify it.

Factory Receive: the Inspector name is auto-filled with your logged-in user — just verify it.

4

Start the trip

Click Start Trip. The trip opens with the scanner, a live manifest, and a batch summary showing scanned / failed counts.

5

Scan pallets one by one

Scan the QR code (or type the number manually) for each pallet. On the return flows each one asks for a condition before adding:

Return Collection: Good / Damaged / Unknown. Choosing Damaged lets you describe it — the system records the pickup and a damage report automatically.

Factory Receive: Good / Damaged. Choosing Damaged sends the pallet to the Damaged status and creates a damage record instead of returning it to Available.

Dispatch: no condition prompt — just click Add to Trip.

6

Watch the manifest update

Every scanned pallet appears with its status change (e.g. Loaded → In Transit). The scanned counter in the header and summary updates instantly.

7

Complete the trip

When all pallets for that load are scanned, click Complete Trip. The trip closes and appears under Recent completed trips.

8

Check the Driver Performance Report

The report now shows a Trips column per driver — how many dispatch trips they completed — alongside Dispatches and Deliveries.

19. Role Flexibility — One User, Multiple Roles

Grant a single account several roles (e.g. Dispatcher + Delivery Receiver) — the user can then perform all of those steps

ℹ️
Why this matters (client Q4)
In practice the same sales team member or truck driver often visits the same customer for a new delivery and picks up returns. With role flexibility that one person can dispatch, confirm delivery, and collect returns — without needing three separate accounts.
1

Open User Management (Administrator only)

Go to Users in the side navigation. Only Administrators can manage users.

2

Add or edit a user and pick multiple roles

Click Add user or the Edit button on a user row. The Roles (multi-select) section lists all 8 roles as checkable chips — tick every role the person needs. Each user row also shows badges for all of the user's roles (e.g. Dispatcher · Delivery Receiver · Return Collector).

The first checked role becomes the account's primary role (used for sorting and the post-login home page).

3

What the combined user can now do

Authorization is the union of every assigned role. A user with Dispatcher + Delivery Receiver + Return Collector can, on the Scan page and Batch Scan page:

  • Dispatch loaded pallets onto a truck (Dispatcher)
  • Confirm delivery at the customer (Delivery Receiver)
  • Collect returns on the return visit (Return Collector)
  • Receive pallets back at the factory (Factory Receiver, if assigned)

The side navigation only shows sections that role set can access (e.g. admin-only Users / Settings stay hidden).

4

Good practice — keep it audited

Every scan and action is still recorded in the Audit Log with the acting user's name and the exact transition, so a combined-role user never works invisibly. Best practice: give combined roles only to trusted team members, and keep Administrator / Manager assignments to a few people.
